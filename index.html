Are the prices right?

They're read from each supermarket's own website on the date shown next to them, and they're big-store prices. Convenience stores like Tesco Express charge more. Lidl doesn't publish prices online, so Lidl is blank until we have shelf photos.

Does it work if I don't eat certain things?

Yes. The first screen asks what you don't eat and hides those recipes. You can change it any time in Settings.

Is it a subscription?

No. £4.99 once. If Fuel ever stops, the unused part is refunded.

Is this medical advice?

No. Protein and calorie figures are estimates from standard ingredient values. See a professional before changing your diet if you have a condition.
