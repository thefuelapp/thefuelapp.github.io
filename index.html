Put it on your phone

Fuel isn't on the App Store yet, so it installs straight from the link. It takes about ten seconds and you get the real thing: its own icon, full screen, and it works in the shop with no signal.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Came from Instagram or TikTok? Tap ··· at the top right, then Open in external browser, so you're in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Fuel on this page.
  3. Tap Share (the square with the arrow). On newer iPhones it's inside the ··· button.
  4. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Close Safari. Open Fuel from the new icon and create your account there.

Do the icon before you sign in: iPhone keeps the home-screen app's sign-in separate from Safari's.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Fuel when the app offers it (or the browser menu → Install app).
Are the prices right?

They're read from each supermarket's own website on the date shown next to them, and they're big-store prices. Convenience stores like Tesco Express charge more. Lidl doesn't publish prices online, so Lidl is blank until we have shelf photos.

Does it work if I don't eat certain things?

Yes. The first screen asks what you don't eat and hides those recipes. You can change it any time in Settings.

Is it a subscription?

No. £4.99 once. If Fuel ever stops, the unused part is refunded.

Is this medical advice?

No. Protein and calorie figures are estimates from standard ingredient values. See a professional before changing your diet if you have a condition.
