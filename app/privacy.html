Privacy note

What we store

Without an account: your plans, pantry and settings stay in the app on your device. The only thing sent is the anonymous step count below, which you can switch off.

With an account: your email address and a password (stored only as a hash by our sign-in provider) and a copy of your app data (plans, pantry, recipe list, settings, targets) so it syncs between your devices. Your bodyweight and goal are part of those settings.

With an account we also keep a small usage note alongside that data: whether Fuel is on your home screen, the kind of phone (iPhone, Android or other), the app version, how many times you've opened it and when you last did. It lets us see whether Fuel is being used. It records nothing about what you look at or tap, and it is never sold or shared.

Counting steps, anonymously

To see where people get stuck, the website and the app count a few steps once per phone: visited the site, opened the app, started set-up, planned a week, made an account, paid, added it to the home screen, shared a week. Each count is stored with a random number made on your phone (not your name, email, plan or anything you typed), the time, and, if you arrived through a tagged link, that tag (for example "tt" for TikTok) or the code in it.

Switch it off any time in Settings → App → "Count my visits, anonymously". Nothing else is counted: not what you look at, tap or plan.

Links with a code

If you came through a friend's or a creator's link (it ends in ?c= and a code), that code is kept with your counts and, if you make an account, with your account. It lets your friend unlock FU£L for free once three people have planned a week with their link, and lets a creator see how many people their link brought and earn a commission on purchases. They see numbers only, never who you are or what you planned.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your data with anyone except the services needed to run Fuel: our database and sign-in provider (Supabase) and, if you buy a pass, the payment provider (Stripe), which handles your card details itself. We never see your card number.

Deleting your data

Sign out and use "Reset everything" to wipe the phone copy. To delete your account and everything stored against it, ask using the contact on the landing page and it will be deleted within 14 days.

Cookies and tracking

FU£L uses only the storage on your phone it needs to work: your saved data, your sign-in session, the random number and link code above, and when your free week started. No tracking cookies, no advertising, no analytics that identify you.
