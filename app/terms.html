Terms of use

Fuel is a meal-planning and shopping-list app. By using it you agree to these terms.

What Fuel is, and isn't

Fuel suggests meals, batch-cooking plans and shopping lists, and estimates their cost and nutrition. It is general information, not medical or dietetic advice. Protein and calorie figures are estimates from standard ingredient values. If you have a medical condition, an allergy, or are pregnant, take advice from a professional before changing your diet. Check ingredient labels yourself for allergens.

Prices

Supermarket prices shown in Fuel are read from each supermarket's own website on the date shown next to them. They are big-store prices, they change without notice, and convenience stores charge more. Fuel does not guarantee any price. The total you pay is the total at the till.

Recipes

Recipes are written for Fuel. Where one is adapted from a public source, the source is linked in the app. You may use them for your own cooking. You may not copy the recipe catalogue or price data to build another product.

Your account and payments

A free account lets you use a limited set of recipes for the current week. Paid passes unlock everything for the period bought. Passes are one-off purchases, not subscriptions, and are non-refundable once used, except where the law says otherwise. If Fuel stops operating during a pass you have paid for, you will be refunded for the unused part.

Your data

Your plans, pantry and settings are stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your account so they sync between devices. See the privacy note.

Availability

Fuel is provided as-is. We try to keep it working and accurate but cannot promise it will always be available or error-free.
