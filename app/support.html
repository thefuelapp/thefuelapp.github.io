Common questions

I paid and the app is still locked. Make sure you are signed in with the same account you paid with, then tap "I've paid, refresh" (website) or "Restore purchases" (iPhone app). If it still does not open, message us with the email on your account.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in form, tap "Forgot password" and follow the link we email you.

A price looks wrong. Prices are read from each supermarket's website on the date shown and change often. Tell us which item and shop and we will check it.

How do I delete my account? In the app: More → Settings → Delete my account. That removes the account and everything saved to it from our server straight away. If you cannot get into the app, message us and we will delete it within 14 days.
