What the week is

21 meals for one person, portions sized for about 2,200 calories and 184g of protein a day: Chilli con carne ×4, Bang bang crispy rice chicken salad ×3, Tuna pasta, cottage cheese tomato sauce ×3, Coconut chicken red curry ×2, Chicken fajitas ×1, Soy garlic honey chicken with rice and broccoli ×1, Skyr protein bowl ×4, Eggs on toast ×3.

The totals are the week’s food. They assume the cupboard basics are already in the house (oil, spices, rice, pasta, sauces, stock cubes), the way they are for anyone past their first shop. With an empty cupboard the first shop costs more at every supermarket; the app shows that figure too.

How the prices were collected

Aldi, ASDA and Sainsbury's prices were read from each supermarket's own website on 20 Sep 2026; Tesco prices on the date shown against them (its website blocks automated reading, so it is refreshed more slowly). These are big-store prices; Tesco Express and Sainsbury's Local charge more. Lidl publishes no prices for its core range anywhere online, so Lidl can't be compared. 'Not stocked' means that shop's website doesn't list the item.

Each shop’s list uses that shop’s own-brand or cheapest standard pack of the same item, and whole packs are counted (you can’t buy half a bag of onions). If a shop doesn’t sell an item, the app prices it at the cheapest other shop and says so. Prices change weekly and vary by store; treat these as a dated snapshot, not a promise. “A uni year” in our posts means 30 weeks.

Other figures we use: a Tesco meal deal is £3.85 with a Clubcard (£4.25 without), as reported by Which? after the 2025 rise. Average student spending (£146 a month on groceries, £48 on takeaways and eating out) is from Save the Student’s National Student Money Survey 2025.
