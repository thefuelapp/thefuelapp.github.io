Creator terms

These are the terms for FU£L's creator programme: you share FU£L, and you earn when people you send buy it. They're short and in plain English. By replying "agreed" to our message, or by sharing your FU£L link, you accept them.

What you get

  • Free FU£L for life. A one-use code that unlocks every feature on your account. It stays yours even if you stop being a creator, unless these terms are broken on purpose (for example fake purchases).
  • A commission on every qualifying purchase through your link or code: £1 per purchase unless we agreed a different amount in writing. Your dashboard always shows your amount.
  • Your own dashboard with live counts: how many people opened your link, planned a week, and bought, and what you've earned and been paid. It never shows who those people are.

What counts as a qualifying purchase

  • Someone pays for FU£L (£4.99, a one-off purchase) and their first visit to FU£L came through your link or your code. It counts whenever they buy, even weeks later, as long as your link was their first.
  • Free weeks, visits and sign-ups don't earn anything. Only purchases do.
  • These don't count: your own purchases; purchases that are refunded; and anything made with fake accounts, bought on someone else's behalf to earn commission, or obtained by paying or rewarding people to buy.
  • Our records (the dashboard, which counts from the payment provider's records) decide which purchases came through you.

When and how you're paid

  • Each purchase is held for 14 days in case of a refund. After that it shows as "ready to pay" on your dashboard.
  • We pay what's ready by UK bank transfer within the first 7 days of each month. We'll ask for your bank details the first time you have something to be paid; send them by email or DM, and we keep them only in our bank's payee list.
  • If a purchase is refunded after we've paid you for it, you keep that payment.
  • You're self-employed for this, not an employee, and you're responsible for any tax on what you earn. (HMRC's £1,000 trading allowance may cover small amounts; check gov.uk if you're not sure.)

Your posts

  • Label every post, story or video about FU£L as an ad. Put #ad at the start of the caption, or use the platform's paid-partnership label. UK advertising rules require it because you get free access and earn commission. If a post isn't labelled, we'll ask you to fix it; if it isn't fixed, we'll pause your code.
  • Only say what's true for you. Show your own week and your own numbers. No made-up savings, results or reviews, no "I've used it for months" if you haven't, and no weight-loss, health or medical promises. If you quote a price, use the one FU£L showed you and say when.
  • Don't share your link in spam, in other people's comments en masse, or in paid search ads that bid on the name FU£L.
  • You're welcome to use FU£L's name, screenshots and screen recordings of the app. Please don't change the FU£L logo.
  • We may share or repost your posts about FU£L on our own accounts, with credit to you. We'll ask first before using your content or face in a paid ad.

Your information

We keep your name, handle, email and code so we can run your link, show your dashboard and pay you. We don't share them with anyone or use them for anything else, and we delete them (apart from payment records we must keep for tax) if you ask us to after you leave. Buyers' details are never shared with you, and yours aren't shown to buyers.

Changes and ending

  • You can stop at any time: just tell us. We'll pay everything that's cleared the 14-day hold, and your free access stays.
  • We can pause or end your code at any time, for example if posts aren't labelled or these terms are broken. Purchases that already counted are still paid, unless they were fake or the terms were broken on purpose.
  • If we change your commission or these terms, we'll message you at least 14 days before. Purchases before the change are paid at the old amount.
  • If FU£L stops operating, we'll pay everything you've earned up to that point.
